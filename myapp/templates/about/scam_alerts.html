Scam Alerts

Protect yourself from common scams on online marketplaces.

⚠️ NEVER send money before receiving items! This is the #1 rule of safe trading.

Common Scams to Watch For

  • Overpayment Scam: Buyer sends a fake cheque for more than the price and asks for change
  • Shipping Scam: Seller asks for payment but never ships the item
  • Phishing: Fake emails pretending to be from TradeX asking for login details
  • Too Good to Be True: Extremely low prices on high-value items

Report Suspicious Activity

If you encounter a scammer, report them immediately to safety@tradex.co.uk
