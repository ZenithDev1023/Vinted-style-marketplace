Safety Tips

Stay safe when buying and selling on TradeX.

🛡️ Meet in Public Places: Always arrange to meet buyers/sellers in busy, public locations.

For Buyers

  • Inspect items before paying
  • Bring a friend when meeting sellers
  • Trust your instincts - if something feels wrong, walk away
  • Never share personal financial information

For Sellers

  • Meet buyers during daylight hours
  • Accept cash or secure payment methods only
  • Don't share your home address publicly
  • Keep records of all transactions
