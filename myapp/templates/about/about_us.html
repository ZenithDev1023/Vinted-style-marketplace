About TradeX

TradeX is the UK's leading marketplace for trade and construction equipment. We connect tradespeople, builders, and DIY enthusiasts to buy, sell, and trade tools, materials, and equipment.

Founded in 2026, our mission is to create a sustainable marketplace where quality tools find new homes and tradespeople can access affordable equipment.

Whether you're a professional contractor or a weekend warrior, TradeX is your go-to platform for all things trade-related.
